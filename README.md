# Coffee-Sales-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide actionable insights into coffee shop performance.

## Overview
The goal of this project is to provide business owners with a clear understanding of their sales patterns. By visualizing transaction data, the dashboard helps identify which products are best-sellers and which times of the day are most profitable.

## Tech Stack
*   Data Visualization: Power BI Desktop
*   Data Transformation: Power Query 
*   Analytics & Metrics: DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
*   Source Data: Exceltransactional records

## Key Features
### 1. KPI Cards
*   **Total Sales:** Aggregated revenue across the selected period.
*   **Total Transactions:** The count of unique sales orders.
*   **Avg Price:** The average unit price across all coffee products sold.

### 2. Interactive Visualizations
*   **Sales by Month (Line Chart):** Identifies seasonal trends and growth patterns over time.
*   **Sales by Time of Day (Donut Chart):** Breaks down revenue into **Morning**, **Afternoon**, and **Night** segments to optimize staffing.
*   **Sales by Products (Clustered Bar Chart):** Compares performance across different menu items to highlight top-selling products.

### 3. Advanced Filtering & Navigation
*   **Search Bar:** Quickly filter data for specific dates.
*   **Date Slicer:** A sleek, button-style calendar slicer for intuitive date range selection.

## Key Insights
*   Monitor peak hours to adjust inventory and staffing levels.
*   Identify underperforming products to refine the menu or marketing strategy.
*   Track monthly revenue targets through the dynamic line chart.
